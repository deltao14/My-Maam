02 — What I Saw

I noticed your hands first and slowly the veins.

The way you moved them across a keyboard — careful, hurried, like you out of patience. Then your calm voice, which made me hear it again and again. Then the quiet way you held a room without asking it to notice you.

If leaving you was a language, I would never learned to speak it. You didn't become my choice — you became my habit of living.

I think you know how much attention it takes to fall for someone slowly. It isn't one big moment. It's a hundred small ones — the patience you give people who don't always deserve it, the softness you never advertise, a loyalty so quiet most people would miss it completely. I didn't.

04 — The Letter

Dear Sujana,

If I could say everything I mean, I don't think I'd need to write it down. But some things sit easier on a page than in a voice that might shake saying them out loud.

You are, quite simply, one of the most sincere people I have known — sincere in your kindness, in your loyalty, even in the things you've never had to say out loud for me to understand them. That kind of heart is rare. Whoever eventually has all of it will be lucky in a way most people never get to be.

I know the last few days have been quiet between us, and I know some of that quiet is mine to own. I'm sorry — plainly, and without expecting anything back for saying it.

But today isn't about any of that. Today is just about you. So — happy birthday, My Maam. I hope this year hands you exactly the kind of joy you deserve, whatever shape it ends up taking. And somewhere in it, I hope you find quiet proof that being this good to people never really goes unnoticed.

— Nayeem
